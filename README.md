🚀 Parshikshana

NEP-Compliant Internship Management & Verification Platform

Parshikshana is a centralized digital platform designed to streamline and strengthen the internship ecosystem in colleges under the NEP 2020 framework.

It connects Students, Colleges, and Industry Partners into a transparent, secure, and scalable internship management system.

⸻

📌 Problem Statement

Under NEP 2020, internships from Semester 5 onward are mandatory for experiential learning. However, the current system faces major challenges:
	•	❌ Lack of industry tie-ups
	•	❌ No proper monitoring or mentorship
	•	❌ Fake internships and certificate mills
	•	❌ Skill gap between students and industry
	•	❌ Poor credit integration with academics
	•	❌ Limited access for rural/semi-urban students

As a result, internships become a formality rather than a meaningful learning experience.

⸻

💡 Solution

Parshikshana provides a technology-driven solution to build a trusted, transparent, and NEP-compliant internship ecosystem.

It ensures:
	•	Verified internships
	•	Real-time monitoring
	•	Credit-based academic integration
	•	Blockchain-backed authenticity
	•	AI-powered student assistance

⸻

🏗️ System Architecture
